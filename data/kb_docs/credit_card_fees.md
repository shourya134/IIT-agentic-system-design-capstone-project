# Credit Card Fee Structure

Credit cards carry an annual joining and renewal fee that varies by card tier, though many entry-level cards waive this fee once annual spending crosses a specified threshold. A late-payment fee is charged when the minimum amount due is not paid by the due date, scaled in slabs based on the outstanding balance, and typically ranges from INR 100 for small balances to INR 1,300 for balances above INR 50,000. Cash withdrawals using a credit card attract a cash-advance fee of around 2.5% of the withdrawn amount, subject to a minimum charge, in addition to interest that accrues from the withdrawal date with no interest-free grace period. Foreign currency transactions incur a currency-conversion markup, usually between 1.99% and 3.5% of the transaction value. Exceeding the assigned credit limit triggers an over-limit fee, generally a fixed amount or a percentage of the excess amount, whichever is higher.
