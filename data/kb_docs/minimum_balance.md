# Minimum Balance Requirements

Savings accounts require the customer to maintain a minimum monthly average balance, which varies by account type and branch location, commonly ranging from INR 1,000 for basic accounts to INR 10,000 or more for premium accounts. Failure to maintain the required average balance results in a non-maintenance charge being deducted at the end of the billing cycle, typically calculated as a percentage of the shortfall rather than a flat fee. Certain account categories, including salary accounts, senior-citizen accounts, and accounts for students, are exempt from minimum balance requirements as long as the associated conditions, such as receiving a monthly salary credit, continue to be met. Customers can avoid the charge by linking a fixed deposit to the savings account or by opting into a zero-balance account variant where eligible. The minimum balance requirement and applicable penalty are disclosed in the account's schedule of charges at the time of account opening.
