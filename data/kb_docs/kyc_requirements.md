# KYC Document Requirements

Every applicant must submit a valid government-issued photo identity proof, such as a PAN card, Aadhaar card, or passport, before any loan or account application can be processed. Address proof is required separately and may be satisfied by a recent utility bill, rental agreement, or Aadhaar card if it carries the current address. Income proof requirements differ by employment type: salaried applicants provide the last three months of salary slips and Form 16, while self-employed applicants provide the latest two years of income tax returns and audited financial statements. A recent passport-sized photograph and a canceled cheque or bank statement for the account to be linked are also mandatory. KYC records must be refreshed periodically, typically every two to ten years depending on the customer's risk classification, and any mismatch between submitted documents and the applicant's stated details results in the application being placed on hold pending re-verification.
