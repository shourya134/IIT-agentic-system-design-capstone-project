# Loan Eligibility Criteria by Loan Type

Personal loan applicants must be salaried or self-employed with a minimum monthly income of INR 25,000 and at least two years of continuous work or business history. Home loan eligibility depends on a stable income source, a loan-to-value ratio capped at 80% of the property's assessed value, and a maximum age of 65 at loan maturity. Auto loan applicants need a minimum credit score of 650 and proof of a stable income covering at least three times the proposed EMI. Education loan eligibility requires admission confirmation from a recognized institution and, for loans above INR 750,000, a co-applicant such as a parent or guardian. Business loans require the applicant's business to have been operational for at least two years with audited financial statements for the most recent fiscal year.
