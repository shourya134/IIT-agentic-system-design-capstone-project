# EMI Calculation Rules

The Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) is calculated using the reducing-balance formula: EMI = P x r x (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n - 1), where P is the principal amount, r is the monthly interest rate, and n is the total number of monthly installments. Interest is charged only on the outstanding principal for that month, so the interest component of each EMI decreases over the loan tenure while the principal component increases. Borrowers may choose a tenure between 12 and 360 months depending on the loan type, and a longer tenure lowers the EMI amount but increases the total interest paid over the life of the loan. Any change to the interest rate, such as a floating-rate reset, triggers a recalculation of the EMI or the remaining tenure, and the borrower is notified of the revised schedule at least 15 days in advance.
