# Fraud and Dispute Resolution Process

A customer who notices an unauthorized transaction or suspects fraudulent activity on their account must report it immediately through the support hotline, mobile app, or a written complaint, ideally within three days of noticing the discrepancy to limit liability. Upon receiving a report, the account or card is temporarily blocked to prevent further unauthorized use while a fraud investigation team reviews transaction logs, device fingerprints, and location data associated with the disputed transaction. The bank is required to provide a provisional resolution, including a provisional credit for eligible unauthorized transactions, within 10 working days of the complaint being logged. The full investigation is typically completed within 90 days, after which the customer receives a written outcome explaining whether the disputed amount will be permanently reversed, partially reversed, or upheld as valid. Customers who disagree with the outcome may escalate the matter to the internal ombudsman or the banking regulator's grievance redressal mechanism.
