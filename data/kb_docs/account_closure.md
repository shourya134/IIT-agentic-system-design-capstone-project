# Account Closure Process

A customer may request account closure by submitting a signed closure form along with their unused chequebook and debit card, either at a branch or through the mobile app for accounts eligible for digital closure. Any outstanding dues, including negative balances, pending loan EMIs linked to the account, or unpaid credit card bills, must be cleared before closure can be completed. Accounts closed within 14 days of opening, sometimes called a cooling-off period, typically do not attract a closure fee, while accounts closed after that window may incur a closure charge depending on the account type and how long it has been open. Any standing instructions, auto-debit mandates, or linked recurring deposits must be canceled or transferred beforehand, since closure automatically terminates them. The customer receives a closure confirmation and a final statement showing the balance transfer or payout of the remaining funds within 7 working days.
