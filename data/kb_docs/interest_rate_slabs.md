# Interest Rate Slabs

Interest rates on loans are assigned in slabs based primarily on the applicant's credit score, with borrowers scoring above 750 typically qualifying for the lowest available rate band for their loan type. Loan-to-value ratio and tenure also influence the slab: shorter tenures and lower loan-to-value ratios generally receive a preferential rate compared to longer tenures or higher-leverage loans. Secured loans such as home and auto loans carry lower rate slabs than unsecured personal loans, reflecting the reduced risk to the lender from the underlying collateral. Rates may be fixed for the full tenure or floating, in which case they are linked to an external benchmark such as the repo rate and reset periodically, typically every three months. Existing customers with a strong repayment history on prior loans may be offered a rate concession of 0.25% to 0.5% below the standard slab for their credit-score band.
